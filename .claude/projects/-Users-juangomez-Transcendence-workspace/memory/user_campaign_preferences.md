User has autism and finds social game design difficult to design/run. Their first campaign module is intentionally focused on exploration and combat with minimal social interaction. The setting supports this — sparse world population, few specimens of each species, not millions.

**Why:** Makes technique and system design that centers exploration/combat more relevant than social mechanics for immediate play. Social system should still exist and be covered, but not as the primary surface for the first module.

**How to apply:** When designing techniques or encounters for the first campaign, prioritize mechanical utility in exploration and creature combat over social or PVP mechanics. Most enemies will be unarmored creatures, not armored humanoids or other players. PVP is rare.
